# Framework Expansion Discussion: Adding Infrastructure & Devices + Learner Readiness

**Date:** May 2026 | **Purpose:** Propose integration of 2 missing dimensions into existing framework

---

## CURRENT STATE: 6 DIMENSIONS

The existing framework has 6 dimensions, each with 5 levels (Confident → Urgent) and multiple observable criteria per level:

| Dimension | Current Focus | # of Sub-Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Staff Digital Capability & Confidence** | M365 skills, teaching with digital, confidence, CPD engagement | 6 sub-criteria |
| **2. HoA Leadership & Planning** | Gap understanding, strategy, QIP/CPD alignment, progress tracking | 5 sub-criteria |
| **3. Digital Skills Assessment & Tracking** | Century IA, specialist skills mapping, progress monitoring, learner visibility | 5 sub-criteria |
| **4. Digital Curriculum Integration** | Assignment design, tool purpose, inclusive access, AT integration | 5 sub-criteria |
| **5. Digital Health (Accessibility & Inclusion)** | WCAG practice, AT awareness, UDL in action, learner confidence | 5 sub-criteria |
| **6. Digital Lead Engagement & Health Checks** | DL identification, Health Check activity, peer coaching, evidence/reporting | 5 sub-criteria |

---

## THE GAP: WHAT'S MISSING

### **Infrastructure & Devices**

**Why it's critical:**
- HoA audit responses repeatedly flagged network/WiFi, device availability, software licensing, hardware as PRIMARY BARRIERS to digital integration
- Cannot teach digital curriculum without reliable infrastructure
- Infrastructure readiness differs by area (workshops vs. classrooms; workshop conditions are different)
- Currently INVISIBLE in the 6 dimensions — no systematic way to assess/track it

**Audit evidence:**
- "Network issues in workshops hinder teaching" (CON/BUI/MOT, CREATIVE_DIGITAL, EEY)
- "Device quantity insufficient" (BUS, MOT)
- "Software licensing gaps block delivery" (5 areas)
- "WiFi unreliable" (3 areas)

**Current placement:** None — NO dimension explicitly covers infrastructure readiness/health

---

### **Learner Readiness & Engagement**

**Why it's critical:**
- Can design beautiful digital curriculum, but if learners lack baseline digital skills or confidence, it won't land
- Learner readiness = entry-point for digital curriculum design (UDL principle: know your audience)
- Currently scattered across multiple dimensions; not coherent
- Dimension 3 touches on "Learner Visibility" (do learners know their level?), but NOT on learner readiness/confidence/digital poverty

**Audit evidence:**
- "Some learners lack laptop access/digital poverty" (CREATIVE_DIGITAL, PAS, others)
- "Learner confidence with systems inconsistent" (CON/BUI/MOT)
- "Learners need foundation skills first" (implied in many responses)

**Current placement:** Fragmented across Dimensions 3 & 4; not systematic

---

## PROPOSAL: TWO MAPPING OPTIONS

### **OPTION A: Create 2 New Dimensions (8 total)**

**Pros:**
- Clean separation of concerns
- Each dimension gets full 5-level treatment with 5+ sub-criteria
- Infrastructure & learner readiness both get visibility/importance they deserve
- Aligns to audit data (these were flagged heavily)

**Cons:**
- Increases complexity from 6D to 8D
- More assessment points for HoAs/DLs (already managing 6 dimensions)
- Longer coaching conversations
- May dilute focus on existing dimensions

**New dimensions would be:**
- **Dimension 7: INFRASTRUCTURE & DIGITAL HEALTH** (Hardware, Network, Software, Device Readiness)
- **Dimension 8: LEARNER READINESS & DIGITAL CONFIDENCE** (Digital poverty, baseline skills, engagement, independence)

---

### **OPTION B: Integrate into Existing Dimensions (6 dimensions, expanded)**

**Sub-option B1: Infrastructure → Dimension 2 (HoA Leadership)**
- Rationale: Infrastructure is a leadership responsibility (budget, planning, escalation)
- Expand Dim 2 from 5 columns → 7 columns (add Infrastructure Planning, Device Strategy)
- Adds 2 columns to the dimension table

**Sub-option B2: Learner Readiness → Dimension 4 (Curriculum Integration)**
- Rationale: Curriculum design MUST respond to learner readiness (UDL principle)
- Expand Dim 4 from 5 columns → 7 columns (add Learner Baseline, Learner Engagement/Confidence)
- Adds 2 columns to the dimension table

**Pros:**
- Keeps framework at 6 dimensions (manageable)
- Reflects systemic integration (leadership + infrastructure; curriculum + learners)
- Reduces assessment points (fewer top-level dimensions)
- Infrastructure sits with strategy/planning (appropriate level)

**Cons:**
- Makes Dimensions 2 & 4 larger (more columns = harder to read)
- Infrastructure slightly "hidden" under leadership (less visible)
- Learner readiness in Curriculum Dim might suggest it's secondary to "integration"
- Dimensions 2 & 4 become complex (multiple distinct topics under one header)

---

### **OPTION C: Hybrid Approach (7 dimensions, mixed placement)**

**Infrastructure → New Dimension 6A (moves current Dim 6 to 7)**
- Rationale: Infrastructure is foundational; deserves its own dimension
- DL Engagement moves to Dimension 7

**Learner Readiness → Expands Dimension 4**
- Rationale: Learners are curriculum participants; assessment/integration/readiness are cohesive
- Adds 2 columns to Dim 4

**Pros:**
- Clean separation: Infrastructure gets its own dimension
- Learner readiness still in context (curriculum integration)
- 7 dimensions is more manageable than 8
- Infrastructure visibility improved

**Cons:**
- Still one dimension shift (Dim 6 → Dim 7)
- Asymmetrical: 1 new dimension, 1 expanded dimension

---

## DETAILED ANALYSIS: WHICH OPTION?

### **My recommendation: OPTION B (Integrate into existing 6D)**

**Why:**

1. **Infrastructure → Dimension 2 (HoA Leadership & Planning)**
   - Infrastructure is fundamentally a LEADERSHIP & PLANNING issue (budget allocation, escalation, strategy)
   - HoA audit responses already flagged infrastructure heavily
   - Makes sense: "Strategy & Ownership" should include infrastructure strategy
   - Expands Dim 2 from: Level | Gap Understanding | Strategy & Ownership | QIP/CPD Alignment | Progress Tracking | DL Action
   - To: Level | Gap Understanding (including infra gaps) | **Infrastructure Strategy** | Strategy & Ownership | QIP/CPD Alignment | Progress Tracking | DL Action
   - **Net: +2 columns** (Gap Understanding splits → Gap Understanding + Infrastructure Assessment; add Infrastructure Strategy)

2. **Learner Readiness → Dimension 4 (Curriculum Integration)**
   - Curriculum design MUST be informed by learner readiness (UDL principle: know your learners)
   - "Integration" means: assignment design + tool choice + accessible access + AT **+ awareness of learner starting point**
   - Expands Dim 4 from: Level | Assignment Design | Tool Purpose | Inclusive Access | AT Integration | DL Action
   - To: Level | Assignment Design | Tool Purpose | **Learner Baseline & Readiness** | Inclusive Access | AT Integration | DL Action
   - **Net: +1 column** (add Learner Baseline & Readiness between Tool Purpose and Inclusive Access)

3. **Benefits:**
   - Keeps framework at 6 dimensions (not overwhelming)
   - Both additions are CONTEXTUALLY INTEGRATED (not bolted-on)
   - Reflects audit findings (infrastructure + learner readiness were top barriers)
   - Manageable table expansion (Dim 2: +2 cols, Dim 4: +1 col)
   - Makes visible that: leadership owns infrastructure, curriculum design responds to learners
