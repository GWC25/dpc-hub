## The Mark Scheme: Clear, Measurable Descriptors

Unlike your previous "gut feeling" RAG, this mark scheme gives you **observable, concrete indicators** for each level:

- **Level 1 (Confident)** — Clear evidence of independent progress, peer mentoring, case study potential
- **Level 2 (On Track)** — Engaged, progressing, specific gaps identified, fortnightly coaching needed
- **Level 3 (Developing)** — Wants to improve, unclear on direction, weekly coaching + targeted training needed
- **Level 4 (Challenged)** — Significant barriers (staff confidence, infrastructure, leadership), intensive support + HoA involvement needed
- **Level 5 (Urgent)** — Fundamental blockage (no DL, no strategy, no ownership), VP Quality escalation needed

## How to Use This: A Coaching Workflow

### **Week 1: Assessment & Alignment**

1. **Schedule coaching conversation** with each audit respondent (HoA + DL together if possible)
2. **Open the Mark Scheme** (Excel Sheet 2)
3. **Rate each dimension** together (1–5): What evidence supports this rating?
4. **Identify the 3 biggest gaps** (dimensions scoring lowest or most critical for learners)

### **Week 2–4: Action & Observation**

1. **Use the Coaching Guide** — pick immediate actions matched to their current level on each dimension
2. **DL runs those actions** (training audit, Century baseline check, Health Check observation, etc.)
3. **Weekly 15-min check-in** with you: what worked? What's blocking? What did we learn?

### **Week 5+: Reflection & Iteration**

1. **Revisit dimensions** — any movement? Any new blockers?
2. **Adjust actions** based on what's working
3. **Celebrate progress** — especially DL effort and peer support
4. **Escalate blockers** — infrastructure gaps, resistance, leadership issues
