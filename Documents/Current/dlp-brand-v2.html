Six Visual Identity Concepts

This document presents six distinct brand identity concepts for the Digital Learning Pedagogy framework at Weston College. Each concept has been developed to reflect the college's Digital Inclusion Framework — Digital Foundations, Accessible Digital Resources, and Inclusive Pedagogy — and the four strategic pillars that guide the Digital Pedagogy Coach role. A central design principle across all concepts is the deliberate avoidance of hierarchy: no element sits "above" or "before" another. All six are presented with rationale for their strategic fit.

Prepared by Graeme Wright · Digital Pedagogy Coach Six concepts follow
Concept 01 of 06 The Ripple
Concept 01 — The Ripple

Every Ring
Reaches Every Other

Digital Learning Pedagogy · Weston College

A stone dropped in water sends ripples outward simultaneously — no ring waits for the one before it to finish. This mark takes the three-layer Digital Inclusion Framework and redraws it as concentric rings rather than a pyramid, preserving the content while eliminating the hierarchy. Digital Foundations, Accessible Resources, and Inclusive Pedagogy are present at every point of the circle. An outer ring adds Capacity and Context — the organisational conditions that enable everything else. The centre is not a destination. It is the source.

Framework Ring 1
Digital Foundations

Essential capabilities, infrastructure, and digital confidence — Teams environments, equipment, digital hours. The conditions before teaching begins.

Framework Ring 2
Accessible Resources

Content and platforms designed to remove barriers. Alt text, captions, assistive technology — equitable access as standard practice, not afterthought.

Framework Ring 3
Inclusive Pedagogy

UDL, hybrid learning, AI-informed assessment. Technology used thoughtfully to meet every learner's needs — the teacher as skilled practitioner.

01
Digital Ecosystem & Standards

Teams health, infrastructure, digital hours — the college-wide baseline

02
Pedagogy, Assessment & Innovation

AI, 21CLD, modelling, learner autonomy — high-impact teaching transformation

03
Inclusion & Accessibility

Assistive tech, accessibility toolkit — accessible by design, not retrofit

04
Capacity Building & QA

Digital Champions, CPD pathways — sustaining impact through people

Strategic Rationale
Why The Ripple works for Weston College
The Ripple directly resolves the concern with the pyramid model: concentric rings carry no implied sequence. A ripple expands in all directions at once — no ring is "above" or "before" another. Staff and learners can engage with inclusive pedagogy at the same time as building digital foundations; the framework accommodates that reality rather than denying it. The mark also communicates scale: a small intervention at the centre sends effects outward to the whole organisation. The teal accent aligns with the digital and wellbeing qualities of the work, while the Weston navy grounds the mark in institutional identity. This concept also reduces well as a standalone icon — the concentric circles remain legible at badge or favicon size without losing meaning.
Concept 02 of 06 The Venn
Concept 02 — The Venn

Where Three
Worlds Meet

Digital Learning Pedagogy · Weston College

A Venn diagram is one of the most universally understood visual arguments: where three circles overlap, something new and distinct emerges. Here, Digital Foundations, Accessible Digital Resources, and Inclusive Pedagogy are equal spheres. None is larger. None sits above the others. They can be entered at any point — but Digital Learning Pedagogy only fully comes to life where all three are simultaneously present. The intersection is not the end of a journey. It is the place where the real work happens.

Equal Sphere
Digital Foundations

Infrastructure, Teams environments, equipment, digital skills — the preconditions for all digital practice. Present and active alongside everything else.

Equal Sphere
Accessible Resources

Content, platforms, and tools designed to remove barriers. SEND support, assistive technology, and accessibility standards applied from the outset.

Equal Sphere
Inclusive Pedagogy

The pedagogical practices — UDL, 21CLD, hybrid delivery — that use technology to ensure every learner can access, engage with and succeed in their education.

01
Digital Ecosystem & Standards

The infrastructure sphere in action across the college

02
Pedagogy, Assessment & Innovation

The pedagogical sphere — AI, modelling, learner autonomy

03
Inclusion & Accessibility

The accessible resources sphere — toolkit, SEND, UDL

04
Capacity Building & QA

The people and governance that sustain all three spheres

Strategic Rationale
Why The Venn works for Weston College
The Venn is the most immediately legible argument against hierarchy available in visual language. Three circles of identical size cannot be ranked. Staff encountering this mark will understand instinctively that digital foundations, accessible resources, and inclusive pedagogy are parallel concerns — not a sequence. Crucially, the golden accent dots at each pairwise intersection are not decorative: they mark where two of the three elements already overlap and create real value, even before all three converge. This gives the mark a second layer of meaning for staff and leaders who engage with it closely. The gold-on-navy palette is distinctive within the Weston colour system without departing from institutional identity. As a logo, the three-circle mark is universally legible at any scale.
Concept 03 of 06 The Compass
Concept 03 — The Compass

Navigate
Your Practice

Digital Learning Pedagogy · Weston College

A compass has no hierarchy. North is no more important than South, East, or West — each direction is equally essential for navigation. Here, the four cardinal points correspond to the four strategic pillars: Ecosystem, Pedagogy, Inclusion, and Capacity. The ordinal points carry the Digital Inclusion Framework's three elements. Digital Learning Pedagogy sits at the centre — not a summit to be reached, but the orientation carried at all times. The strapline Navigate your practice is purposeful, professional, and applicable across all CPD and staff-facing communications.

Ordinal Point NE / NW
Digital Foundations

The preconditions of all digital practice — infrastructure, digital skills, Teams environments, and the baseline equipment audit across the college.

Ordinal Point SE / SW
Accessible Resources

Platforms and content that remove barriers by design — accessibility standards, assistive technology, and SEND-informed approaches embedded throughout.

Cardinal Points N / S / E / W
The Four Strategic Pillars

Ecosystem & Standards, Pedagogy & Innovation, Inclusion & Accessibility, and Capacity Building & QA — equal directions, all navigated from a shared centre.

N
Digital Ecosystem & Standards

Teams, equipment, digital hours — the northern bearing of practice

E
Pedagogy, Assessment & Innovation

AI, 21CLD, modelling — the eastern direction of professional growth

W
Inclusion & Accessibility

Assistive tech, SEND, UDL — the western commitment to equity

S
Capacity Building & QA

Champions, CPD, governance — the southern foundation of sustainability

Strategic Rationale
Why The Compass works for Weston College
The compass is perhaps the most powerful metaphor for professional development: it provides orientation, not a fixed route. Staff at different stages of their digital practice will be travelling in different directions — and the compass affirms that all directions are valid. The strapline "Navigate your practice" positions Digital Learning Pedagogy as a set of tools for professional agency rather than a top-down mandate. For leadership reporting, the four cardinal points map cleanly onto the four strategic pillars, making this mark simultaneously a logo and a planning framework. The dark-ground treatment aligns with modern digital presentation environments (Teams backgrounds, projected slides) without requiring colour printing. This concept has the strongest potential as a staff-facing brand identity across lanyards, CPD materials, and communications.
Concept 04 of 06 The Orbit
Concept 04 — The Orbit

Drawn Together,
Not Stacked Up

Digital Learning Pedagogy · Weston College

In a solar system, the sun does not rank above the planets — it simply holds them in relation to each other through gravity. Here, Digital Learning Pedagogy is the gravitational centre: it draws the four strategic pillars and the three framework elements into relationship without ordering them. Each orbiting body is complete in itself; it is their collective arrangement that creates the system. The orbit is also inherently dynamic — things in orbit are always in motion, always returning, never finished.

Inner Orbit
Digital Foundations

The closest orbit — always proximate, always influencing. Infrastructure, digital skills, and Teams environments that enable everything else in the system.

Inner Orbit
Accessible Resources

Content and tools in close orbit around the pedagogical centre — accessible by design and always within reach, for every learner and practitioner.

Outer Orbit
The Four Strategic Pillars

Ecosystem, Pedagogy, Inclusion, and Capacity orbit at the same radius — equal distance from the centre, none privileged above the others.

01
Digital Ecosystem & Standards

In orbit — always returning, always in motion

02
Pedagogy, Assessment & Innovation

In orbit — AI, 21CLD, modelling, learner autonomy

03
Inclusion & Accessibility

In orbit — assistive tech, SEND, accessible toolkit

04
Capacity Building & QA

In orbit — Champions, CPD pathways, quality governance

Strategic Rationale
Why The Orbit works for Weston College
The orbital model communicates two things that no pyramid can: everything moves, and the centre holds. Digital Learning Pedagogy as a gravitational centre is a compelling reframe — it doesn't sit at the top of a hierarchy, it holds everything in productive relationship. The four strategic pillars orbiting at equal radius makes explicit their equivalence as priorities. The inclined ellipses reflect the reality that different work sits at different angles — there is no single correct plane of operation. The dark, deep-space aesthetic is immediately distinctive and would differentiate Weston's Digital Learning Pedagogy identity from any comparable programme in the FE sector.
Concept 05 of 06 The Constellation
Concept 05 — The Constellation

Every Star
Shines Independently

Digital Learning Pedagogy · Weston College

Stars in a constellation do not rank each other. Each one burns independently, with its own intensity and meaning. Together, they form a pattern that practitioners use to navigate — to orient themselves, to find their position, to plan their journey. The constellation names the stars: Digital Foundations, Accessible Resources, Inclusive Pedagogy, and the four strategic pillars. Digital Learning Pedagogy is the brightest star at the centre — visible in all conditions, the point everything else orients around. The connecting lines are not dependencies. They are relationships.

Named Star
Digital Foundations

Essential capabilities, infrastructure, and confidence — the base stars that anchor the whole constellation in educational reality.

Named Star
Accessible Resources

Platforms, content, and assistive technology designed to remove barriers — a star that shines for every learner, not just the majority.

Named Star
Inclusive Pedagogy

UDL, hybrid delivery, AI-informed practice — the pedagogical star that transforms what is delivered into what is actually learned.

01
Digital Ecosystem & Standards

A named star — Teams, equipment, the college-wide baseline

02
Pedagogy, Assessment & Innovation

A named star — AI, 21CLD, modelling, autonomous learning

03
Inclusion & Accessibility

A named star — assistive tech, SEND, the toolkit

04
Capacity Building & QA

A named star — Champions, CPD, quality governance

Strategic Rationale
Why The Constellation works for Weston College
The constellation metaphor resolves the hierarchy question through a different logic: stars are not ranked, they are named. The act of naming a star — Foundations, Accessibility, Pedagogy — is an act of recognition, not comparison. This makes the framework particularly powerful as a staff engagement tool: it invites staff to identify which star they are working at rather than which "level" they have reached. Constellations are also fundamentally about navigation: they exist to help people find their way. The visual identity is premium, distinctive, and immediately differentiates Weston's Digital Learning Pedagogy from anything in the FE sector. The deep-space treatment works exceptionally well on digital screens, Teams backgrounds, and projected presentations.
Concept 06 of 06 The Prism
Concept 06 — The Prism

What Was Always
There, Revealed

Digital Learning Pedagogy · Weston College

A prism does not add colour to white light — it reveals the full spectrum that was already present. Digital Learning Pedagogy works in exactly this way: it does not replace excellent teaching and learning; it reveals the depth, range, and capacity that already exists within it. The three faces of the prism are the three layers of the Digital Inclusion Framework. Light enters as Teaching and Learning; it leaves as four distinct, visible strategic pillars. The transformation is not hierarchical. It is optical — inevitable, simultaneous, and complete.

Left Face
Digital Foundations

The first surface light meets — essential infrastructure, digital skills, and Teams environments. The physical face of the prism that makes refraction possible.

Right Face
Accessible Resources

The second surface — content, accessibility standards, and assistive technology. The refracting face that ensures the emerging spectrum reaches every learner.

Base Face
Inclusive Pedagogy

The third face — UDL, hybrid delivery, AI-informed practice. The base on which the whole optical structure rests, orientating everything toward learner need.

01
Digital Ecosystem & Standards

The first refracted beam — infrastructure and standards made visible

02
Pedagogy, Assessment & Innovation

The second beam — AI, 21CLD, and modelling in distinct focus

03
Inclusion & Accessibility

The third beam — SEND, assistive tech, and equitable design

04
Capacity Building & QA

The fourth beam — Champions, CPD, and quality governance

Strategic Rationale
Why The Prism works for Weston College
The prism offers a conceptually distinct argument that no other metaphor makes: Digital Learning Pedagogy reveals what was already present in great teaching. This is a respectful and affirming message for staff — it positions digital development not as an external imposition but as a clarifying lens on existing expertise. The incoming beam labelled "TLA" is crucial: it grounds the metaphor in the college's existing language and quality framework. Refraction is simultaneous — all four pillar beams emerge at the same moment, not in sequence — which preserves the non-hierarchical principle. The prism mark is also uniquely bold as a print or screen identity: clean geometric lines that reproduce equally well in full colour, two-colour, or single-colour applications. The four coloured beams directly reflect the colour-coding already in use in the Digital Impact Hub, providing natural system coherence.
