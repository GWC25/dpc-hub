Section 1

Purpose & Scope

How Weston College will build and sustain staff digital capability across all curriculum areas — two interconnected tracks, one coherent programme.

This plan operates across two interconnected tracks:

Track 1 · Digital Leads

Named staff (one per curriculum area) who act as the embedded digital development contact for their team, run Teams Health Checks, and support peer upskilling. This is the primary focus of the plan.

Track 2 · All Teaching & Support Staff

A broader expectation of digital skills progression, tracked via the Jisc Discovery Tool for baseline and Microsoft Learn, LinkedIn Learning, or Jisc pathways for development.

Learner digital skills — separate framework

Learner digital skills are addressed in Section 5, using Century as the primary tracking platform for General Digital Skills, alongside area-specific Specialist Digital Skills mapping agreed with each HoA.

Section 2

Design Principles

Seven principles that shape every decision in this programme — from platform choice to accountability model.

Low burden

All CPD resources are free-to-access and self-paced. Core platforms: Microsoft Learn, Jisc BDC (institutional subscription), LinkedIn Learning (via Microsoft 365). No additional cost to the college.

Evidence-based

Every pathway produces a verifiable output — a completion certificate, module badge, or line-manager-signed log entry. Teach Meet engagement and 1:1 coaching sessions count as evidence.

Rooted in practice

Development is tied directly to what Digital Leads need to do in their area. The 1:1 coaching model (DPC → Digital Lead → team) and Teach Meet network ensure CPD translates into changed practice.

Accountable through line management

Digital Leads report progress to their HoA. Individual Jisc results are private — progress is reported in aggregate and through line manager sign-off at review points.

Progressive

Pathways align to the Digital Learning Pyramid (Foundations → Inclusion → Innovation). Discovery Tool question sets are sequenced across the year for multiple comparable data points.

Equity-aware

Access must not depend on premium personal accounts. All platforms have free or institution-licensed tiers. LinkedIn Learning is accessed via the Microsoft 365 integration.

Institutionally grounded

Weston College has a published Jisc case study (March 2021) documenting the college's earlier Discovery Tool implementation with staff and learners. This programme builds directly on that foundation — it is a continuation and scaling of that work, not a new initiative.

For senior leaders — Jisc Digital Elevation Tool

Separate from this plan, Jisc offers a Digital Elevation Tool for senior strategic FE leaders — a self-assessment against five themes (learner experience, curriculum development, staff experience, leadership/culture/governance, underpinning technologies) mapped against a pre-foundation → elevate model. Recommended action for Neil Davies / Ben Manning: complete this as a strategic planning input for 2026/27.

Section 3

Digital Leads Programme

The core of the programme — 14 named Leads in post, 20 areas still to identify, and a three-phase CPD pathway differentiated by RAG.

3.1 What is a Digital Lead?

A Digital Lead is a teaching or support staff member, nominated within each curriculum area, who takes on a defined set of digital responsibilities alongside their main role. This is not a separate management grade — it is a recognised professional contribution. Digital Leads are distinct from Digital Champions, which is a separate learner-facing initiative for a future phase.

3.2 Role Responsibilities

Teams Digital Health Check

Run the Health Check for their area termly (once trained). Share results with their HoA.

First point of contact

Triage digital queries within their team before escalating to the DPC.

Good practice sharing

Signpost colleagues to resources, lead short Teach Meet slots — at least once per year.

QIP & Business Plan support

Support the HoA to complete digital data in Business Plans and Quality Improvement Plans.

CPD pathway

Complete the Digital Lead CPD pathway (see Section 3.4) and maintain a brief evidence log.

Network meetings

Attend Digital Leads network meetings — one per term, facilitated by the DPC.

Section 4

All-Staff Digital Skills Development

Light-touch administration, credible governance evidence, and a Jisc baseline that respects staff privacy — delivered through existing line management structures.

4.1 Rationale

Digital upskilling for all teaching staff is increasingly central to the college's quality agenda. As accountability for learner digital skills grows — moving towards parity with English and Maths expectations in funded programmes — staff confidence must keep pace. The college cannot hold learners to a standard that staff have not themselves reached.

4.2 Baseline: Jisc Building Digital Capability & Discovery Tool

The Jisc BDC service and its Discovery Tool provide the validated, sector-standard baseline for staff digital capability. Weston College has an institutional subscription covering both staff and learner use. The Discovery Tool is the recommended starting point for all teaching staff.

  • Individual results remain private to the staff member — line managers and the DPC do not see personal scores.
  • TLAMs record the number of staff who have completed (Y/N only) — not scores.
  • Aggregate area-level data is shared with the DPC termly and used to target CPD offers.
  • LinkedIn Learning resources are mapped directly to the Jisc BDC framework — specific content can be recommended based on a staff member's Discovery Tool profile.

4.2a Discovery Tool Question Sets — Recommended Sequence

Setting all question sets at once overwhelms staff and produces a single data point with no year-on-year comparison. The recommended approach sequences three sets across the year, generating three comparable data points for governance reporting.

4.3a Teach Meets & 1:1 Coaching — The Integration Layer

Platform-based CPD on its own does not change teaching practice. These mechanisms are what make development stick.

Teach Meets

Short practitioner-led sessions (10–15 min), termly, DPC-facilitated. Staff share one digital approach that has worked in their area. Digital Leads lead at least once per year. Themes curated from Discovery Tool aggregate data.

Attendance log · Presenter record · Feeds Staff Capability & Confidence KPIs

1:1 Coaching

Focused sessions (30–60 min) addressing a specific digital challenge in the staff member's teaching context. Triggered by: Digital Lead referral, TLAM request, or Learning Walk finding. DPC logs all sessions.

Coaching log entry · Feeds Teaching Quality Indicators & Value for Money KPIs

Digital Leads peer support

Digital Leads act as first point of contact for digital queries in their team, triaging before escalating to the DPC. Extends the reach of the coaching model without increasing the DPC's direct caseload.

Lead activity log · Evidences distributed capacity building
Tracking model — needs refinement and approval

Individual Jisc data is not centrally accessible by design (GDPR). TLAMs record completion numbers (Y/N, not scores). Staff log module completions co-signed by their line manager. This approach needs to be refined and approved with Neil Davies before implementation.

Section 5

Learner Digital Skills

Century for General Digital Skills tracking, plus area-specific Specialist Digital Skills mapping agreed with each HoA.

5.1 Two-Track Model

General Digital Skills

Tracked via Century. Provides an Initial Assessment (IA) placing learners at Entry 3 or Level 1, followed by diagnostic assessments and targeted nugget-based learning.

Specialist Digital Skills

Industry-specific tools and practices defined collaboratively with HoAs. Tracked through bespoke mapping agreed between the DPC and each HoA, evidenced through portfolios and learning walk observations.

Jisc Discovery Tool vs Century — important distinction

The Jisc Discovery Tool is for teaching staff — it measures digital capability against the DTPF framework. Century is for learners — it measures General Digital Skills (aligned to EDS / DigComp). They serve different purposes and different audiences. Both should be in use; they should not be conflated or used interchangeably.

5.2 Century: General Digital Skills Model

Initial Assessment
All learners complete the Century IA at enrolment. Places them at Entry 3 or Level 1. Results visible to teaching team and DPC via the Century dashboard.
Diagnostic assessments
Teachers or DPC set up diagnostics by theme throughout the year (digital communication, data handling, online safety). Identifies specific gaps within a learner's level.
Nugget learning
Learners are assigned or self-select Century 'nuggets' — short, adaptive learning units — to address gaps. Progress is automatically tracked in the Century dashboard.
Progress review
Minimum termly: teachers and DPC review Century dashboard data to identify learners making insufficient progress. Interventions planned with HoA.
Absent learners
Century content remains accessible asynchronously via Teams. Teachers share resources in advance. Progress captured via Century dashboard.
Train the Trainer
DPC arranges Century (Sam) → Libby Heath-Tavener (E&M lead) → E&M team training. DPC supports and extends model to other areas.

Section 8

Integration with the College Quality Cycle

This programme generates quality evidence as a byproduct of doing the work — not as a parallel data collection exercise. Digital pedagogy development is not a separate workstream: it is the quality cycle, seen through a digital lens.

8.1 Quality Processes — Digital Connection Map

Click each process to expand the detail.

🚶
Learning Walks & Developmental Observations
Three windows: Sept–Nov · Jan–Mar · Apr–Jun · All teaching staff: minimum 1 per year
▾
How digital development connects

LW narratives explicitly assess Teams environments, accessible resources, and whether digital tools purposefully change the learning experience. The DPC co-designs digital observation criteria with TLAMs and can support or co-conduct Developmental Observations for referred staff.

Evidence generated

LW narrative excerpts referencing digital TLA feed the Teaching Quality Indicators KPI. Before/after comparison between areas with and without a trained Digital Lead evidences programme impact. DPC logs all LW conversations involving digital coaching referrals.

🔍
Quality Review Activity (QRA)
Risk/volume basis throughout the year · One week's notice given
▾
How digital development connects

QRAs replicate Ofsted EIF methodology and explicitly scrutinise learning environments including digital resources, Teams environments, and learner evidence of digital skills. Areas with trained Digital Leads and completed Health Checks should demonstrate visibly stronger digital environments.

Evidence generated

QRA findings referencing digital environment quality feed directly into HoA RAG ratings and DPC coaching priorities. Areas flagged in QRAs are prioritised for DPC intervention. Improvement between QRAs is a measurable programme outcome.

📋
Programme Performance Reviews (PPRs)
Three times per year: Oct · Feb · May · ~1 hour per programme
▾
How digital development connects

PPRs are the formative self-assessment tool for each programme. Century diagnostic data, Jisc Discovery Tool progress, Teams Assignment analytics, and learner digital skills tracking all become PPR evidence. The DPC provides area-level digital data summaries in advance of each PPR window on request.

Evidence generated

PPR records referencing digital skills data evidence QIP Traction and Student Experience KPIs. DPC digital data summaries create a documented service record. Improvement trends between Term 1 and Term 3 PPRs show programme impact within a single academic year.

🏛
Curriculum Quality Review Panels (CQRPs)
Bi-annual: Nov–Dec (Term 1) · Mar–Apr (Term 2) · Chaired by VP Quality
▾
How digital development connects

CQRP agenda item 7 is explicitly "TLA Overview — Self, Peer, QRAs." Digital coaching records, HoA RAG ratings, and Digital Lead action plans feed this item directly. The DPC contributes a written summary to CQRPs for areas where digital development has been a focus.

Evidence generated

CQRP records documenting digital TLA strengths and AFIs provide panel-level evidence for Teaching Quality Indicators. Cross-college themes identified at CQRP (e.g. Teams environment consistency, accessibility gaps) inform the DPC's Teach Meet programme for the following term.

📈
Quality Improvement Plans (QIPs)
Reviewed three times per year in line with PPR and CQRP windows
▾
How digital development connects

Each area's QIP includes a CPD tab linking staff development to TLA impact. Digital skills AFIs identified through HoA meetings, LWs, or QRAs are translated into QIP actions by the DPC in collaboration with TLAMs and HoAs. The DPC supports TLAMs to articulate measurable digital improvement targets.

Evidence generated

Number of QIPs with digital inclusion actions is a direct KPI measure (QIP Traction). Completion rate of those actions at each review point evidences programme effectiveness. QIP actions referencing Century or Jisc data connect individual area improvement to college-wide digital capability data.

💬
Student Voice & Student Surveys
First Impressions: Nov–Dec · End of Year: Apr–May · Apprentice Survey: Jan–Apr
▾
How digital development connects

One of the five key college survey questions is "How would you rate the quality of teaching on your course?" Digital TLA confidence, accessible resources, and well-structured Teams environments directly affect this score. Century progress data complements survey results by showing digital skills progression, not just satisfaction.

Evidence generated

Student survey scores on teaching quality cross-referenced with areas where the DPC has been active provide a learner-experience measure of programme impact. Century dashboard data provides a quantitative complement to the qualitative survey. Apprenticeship survey data is particularly relevant for areas using Smart Assessor and EPA portfolios.

Self-Assessment Report (SAR)
Annual: Draft by November · Sign-off by December
▾
How digital development connects

The DPC supports the digital skills dimension of the annual SAR, drawing on aggregate data from Jisc Discovery Tool (staff baseline and progress), Century (learner digital skills), HoA RAG ratings, LW and QRA evidence, and coaching logs. Evidence generated

The SAR digital dimension is itself an evidence output of the programme. Year-on-year improvement in the SAR digital narrative — supported by the data sources above — is the highest-level evidence of sustained programme impact. The DPC contributes a written summary to the Quality Team for SAR compilation.
