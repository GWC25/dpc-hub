# COACHING GUIDE: Multi-Dimensional RAG Assessment Framework

## Why 6 Dimensions, Not Just One RAG?

A single RAG rating tells you **what**, but not **where to look or what lever to pull**. This framework operationalizes the Digital Learning Pyramid into **six measurable dimensions**. Together, they tell you:

1. **Where the area actually is** (not just your gut feeling)
2. **Which specific levers will move progress** (not generic "digital training")
3. **What the Digital Lead should do this week** (not someday)

This prevents assessment becoming **"weighing the pig"** — data collection with no action.

---

## The 6 Dimensions

### **Dimension 1: Staff Digital Capability & Confidence**
**The building block.** Can teachers reliably use M365? Do they understand accessibility? Are they confident with emerging tools?

**Aligned to:** ETF DTPF Stages 1–3 (Exploring → Adopting → Leading)  
**Data source:** Jisc Discovery Tool results (aggregate), one-to-one conversations, Learning Walk observations  
**Question:** "On the Pyramid, where are the teaching staff in this area?"

---

### **Dimension 2: HoA Leadership Understanding & Planning**
**The difference between awareness and ownership.** Does the Head of Area own the digital strategy, understand gaps, have a credible plan, and track progress?

**Aligned to:** Quality cycle, QIP, CPD levers in Quality Handbook  
**Data source:** HoA conversations, QIP review, budget allocation, CPD calendar  
**Question:** "Does the HoA own digital improvement or is it something the DPC does?"

---

### **Dimension 3: Digital Skills Assessment & Tracking**
**The evidence.** Are learner digital skills baseline captured at entry? Are specialist skills mapped and tracked? Is progress embedded in curriculum tracking systems?

**Aligned to:** Century Tech EDS diagnostics, specialist skills mapping, Markbook integration  
**Data source:** Century dashboard, HoA specialist skills tracking table, course tracking documentation  
**Question:** "Can you pull a report showing each learner's digital skills starting point and progress?"

---

### **Dimension 4: Digital Curriculum Integration**
**Pedagogy, not tools.** Are digital skills woven into teaching or bolted on? Are assignment designs inclusive? Is AT promoted proactively?

**Aligned to:** UDL principles, QFT, Digital Learning Pyramid Level 2 (Inclusion)  
**Data source:** Assignment review, Learning Walk observations, feedback on accessible design  
**Question:** "When you look at a unit of work, is digital essential or optional?"

---

### **Dimension 5: Digital Health Observations (Accessibility & Inclusion)**
**The practice reality.** Learning Walk data showing accessible practice: WCAG 2.2 AA resources, AT awareness, UDL, inclusive classroom culture.

**Aligned to:** Learning Without Barriers (the thread through all Pyramid levels)  
**Data source:** Learning Walks, Digital Health Check observations, student feedback on accessibility  
**Question:** "Can you see inclusion in action in a learning session?"

---

### **Dimension 6: Digital Lead Engagement & Health Checks**
**The sustainability lever.** Is the DL identified, engaged, running Health Checks, coaching peers, and gathering evidence?

**Aligned to:** Digital Leads Training Plan 2025–27, Health Check framework  
**Data source:** DL engagement conversation, Health Check schedule, peer coaching observations  
**Question:** "Is the DL your eyes and ears in the area, or waiting for guidance?"

---

## Using the Mark Scheme (Sheet 2)

Each dimension has **clear, measurable, observable descriptors** at Levels 1–5:

### **Level 1 — Confident**
Area is progressing independently. DL can mentor peers. Document for case study.

### **Level 2 — On Track**
Area is engaged and making progress. Some specific gaps. Fortnightly coaching needed.

### **Level 3 — Developing**
Area wants to improve but lacks clarity or structure. Weekly coaching or training pivots needed.

### **Level 4 — Challenged**
Area has significant barriers (staff confidence, infrastructure, leadership ownership). Intensive support required. HoA partnership essential.

### **Level 5 — Urgent**
Area leadership unclear on digital strategy or lacking ownership. Structural barriers present. VP Quality involvement needed.

## The "Weighting" Question

You asked: *How do you combine the 6 dimensional RAGs into an overall RAG?*

**There is no formula.** The overall RAG is a **judgment call after looking at all six dimensions**, but here's a rough guide:

- **If 4+ dimensions at Level 1–2:** Overall = **Level 1–2 (Confident/On Track)**
- **If 3–4 dimensions at Level 2–3, no Level 5s:** Overall = **Level 3 (Developing)**
- **If 2+ dimensions at Level 4–5, or one critical Level 5:** Overall = **Level 4–5 (Challenged/Urgent)**

But the power is **not in the overall number**. The power is **seeing which dimensions are holding progress back** and **knowing exactly what to do about them**.

---

## When to Escalate

If an area scores **Level 4–5** on **any** of these dimensions, flag it:

- **Dimension 1 (Staff Capability) = Level 5** → Intensive support plan with TLAM
- **Dimension 2 (HoA Leadership) = Level 5** → VP Quality conversation; strategic partnership agreement
- **Dimension 5 (Accessibility Health) = Level 4–5** → Urgent accessibility teach-meet + Liz Burkley/Lois Knight partnership
- **Dimension 6 (DL Engagement) = Level 5** → No DL identified → work with HoA to identify one immediately

---

## The Thread Through Everything: Learning Without Barriers

At every level of the Pyramid, accessibility and inclusion should be visible:

- **Level 1 (Foundations):** Every staff member knows Immersive Reader exists; every resource passes accessibility checker
- **Level 2 (Inclusion):** AT offered proactively to all; UDL principles embedded; inclusive design is non-negotiable
- **Level 3 (Innovation):** Staff advocate for AT investment; champion WCAG 2.2 AA as institutional standard; shape policy on digital access

If **Dimension 5 (Accessibility Health) is significantly lower** than other dimensions, it's a signal: digital practice is happening, but may not be inclusive. This is the DPC's red flag to intervene.
