# FOR DIGITAL LEADS: How to Use This Framework with Your Area

## What You're Looking At

Graeme (your Digital Pedagogy Coach) has created a **six-dimension assessment framework** to help you understand **exactly where your area is** and **what to do next** to move progress forward. This is not compliance. It's a coaching tool designed to help you drive impact.

## The 6 Dimensions (In Plain English)

| **Dimension** | **Simple Question** | **Your Role** |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Staff Digital Capability** | Can your team reliably use M365 and accessibility tools? Are they confident? | Identify who needs what training. Mentor peers. Demo tools. |
| **2. HoA Leadership** | Does your Head of Area own the digital strategy and track progress? | Help HoA see gaps clearly. Bring data. Keep them informed. |
| **3. Digital Skills Assessment** | Do you know where each learner is starting and how they're progressing? | Check Century dashboard. Map specialist skills. Track progress. |

| **5. Accessibility & Inclusion Health** | Can you see inclusive practice in action (WCAG AA, AT, UDL)? | Observe learning sessions. Note what's working/what needs improvement. |
| **6. Digital Lead Engagement** | Are you engaged, visible, gathering evidence, coaching peers? | Run Health Checks. Mentor. Report back to DPC weekly. |

## What Does "Health Check" Mean?

A **Digital Health Check** is a **30-minute structured observation** of one learning session. You're looking for:

✓ **WCAG 2.2 AA practice**: Are resources accessible? Alt text on images? Color contrast okay?  
✓ **AT Awareness**: Do learners know about Immersive Reader, Live Captions, accessibility checker?  
✓ **Inclusive Design**: Can all learners access the activity/assignment equally?  
✓ **Learner Independence**: Can learners navigate digital tools without constant teacher prompting?  
✓ **Digital Purpose**: Is technology essential to the learning, or could it be done without it?

**What you do:**
1. Sit in on a learning session (class, workshop, online)
2. Observe quietly using the observation proforma
3. Note specific examples (not judgments)
4. Thank the teacher; share one thing that went well + one suggestion
5. Write up findings; share with Graeme

**What you're NOT doing:**
- Evaluating the teacher
- Judging delivery quality
- Collecting data for performance management

You're being **Graeme's eyes and ears**. That's it.

## The Mark Scheme: What Each Level Means for You

### **Level 1 — Confident**
Your area is moving forward independently. You're ready to **mentor peers from other areas**. Document what you're doing as a case study for the college.

**Your action:** Be a champion. Help others do what you're doing.

---

### **Level 2 — On Track**
Your area is engaged and progressing. You have some specific gaps to close. You need **regular coaching** (fortnightly check-ins with Graeme).

**Your action:** Identify the specific gap. Agree on ONE thing to fix next. Get peer support.

---

### **Level 3 — Developing**
Your area wants to improve but isn't quite clear on direction or doesn't have the resources yet. You need **weekly coaching + targeted training**.

**Your action:** Help your HoA see the gaps clearly. Ask for what you need. Try one small thing.

---

### **Level 4 — Challenged**
Significant barriers exist (staff confidence, infrastructure, leadership ownership, time). You need **intensive support** and your HoA needs to be involved in problem-solving.

**Your action:** Name the barrier honestly. Don't try to fix this alone. Escalate.

---

### **Level 5 — Urgent**
Something fundamental is blocking progress (no DL identified, no strategy, no leadership ownership). This needs **senior leader intervention**.

**Your action:** Flag immediately to Graeme. This requires HoA + VP Quality conversation.

## Key Principles

### **1. This is about impact, not just data**

The goal is not to collect more numbers. It's to **understand what will actually move progress in your area**. If a dimension shows Level 3 (Developing), the follow-up question is: "What's the smallest thing we can do this week that will matter?"

### **2. You are not the expert on everything**

- Infrastructure barriers? Escalate to HoA + IT
- Staff confidence gaps? Work with TLAM or Graeme
- Assessment design? Collaborate with peers
- Accessibility barriers? Involve Liz Burkley or Lois Knight

Your role is to **spot what's happening and connect the right support**.

### **3. Learning Without Barriers runs through everything**

If you notice:
- Resources not accessible (no captions, no alt text, poor color contrast)
- AT not offered proactively (only to learners with "recorded need")
- Inclusive design not considered

…then **Dimension 5 is your red flag**. Flag it to Graeme. It matters for all learners, not just SEND.
